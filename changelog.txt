26.1.2-0.12.0

- The mutability crop stat now does something: it raises the chance that pollen takes hold, up to 3x at max mutability. Pollen is consumed even when a mutation fails to take, so crops with low mutability are harder and more expensive to mutate
- Lowered the base mutation chance on all pollination recipes, so mutating crops is more of a grind and leveling up mutability matters more
- Bees can now cross-pollinate and mutate supported external crops (vanilla and modded), not just Productive Farming's own crops


26.1.2-0.11.0

- Added Productive Trees compat: the Farm Controller now harvests ripe fruit from fruit trees
- Nutrient water now accelerates fruit tree growth as well as crops
- Harvested fruit is collected straight into the controller instead of dropping on the ground
- The Farm Controller now stops harvesting crops and fruit while its output is full, instead of harvesting and destroying the overflow
- Lowered nutrient water usage per growth tick from 100 to 50 mB


26.1.2-0.10.0

- Reworked the AgriTech and AgriTech: Evolved integration to use AgriTech's official events instead of mixins
- Crop stats now show in Jade for AgriTech: Evolved's Advanced Planter
- The yield stat no longer inflates seed drops
- Fixed vertical trellis crops (vanilla, cucumber, zucchini, luffa and more) dropping nothing and not gaining stats when harvested
- Fixed the Farm Controller's brick faces not lining up with the multiblock when rotated
- Fixed the Crop Mutation and Crop Fruiting JEI recipe backgrounds rendering squished in the corner


26.1.2-0.9.0

- Crop stat increase chance is now fully data-driven through the stat_increase_chance datamap replacing the config
- Mystical Agriculture seeds are grouped into mystical_tier_1 through mystical_tier_5 tags, with higher tiers given a lower stat increase chance (0.05 down to 0.01)
- Fixed Mystical Agriculture seeds not keeping their crop stats when harvested
- Harvested vanilla and modded crop seeds now always carry crop stats
- Fixed only one input slot being usable in the Farm Controller
